# Diwali Sales Data Analysis

## Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a Diwali sales dataset to understand customer purchasing behavior, sales patterns, and product performance.

The analysis uses Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to identify meaningful patterns across customer demographics, geographic regions, occupations, and product categories.

The project demonstrates a complete EDA workflow, including **data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, visualization, and business insights generation**.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Clean and preprocess the Diwali sales dataset
- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior
- Identify important customer segments
- Analyze sales across different states
- Study purchasing patterns across age groups and genders
- Analyze sales based on customer occupation
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Visualize important patterns and relationships
- Generate actionable insights from the dataset

## Key Findings

The EDA indicates several notable patterns:

- **Female customers** account for a substantial share of observed purchases.
- Customers in the **26–35 age group** represent an important purchasing segment.
- **Married women** account for a significant share of the observed purchases.
- **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka** contribute significantly to overall sales.
- Customers working in **IT, Healthcare, and Aviation** show relatively high purchase activity.
- Different customer demographics and geographic regions show noticeable differences in purchasing behavior.

---

## Conclusion

The exploratory data analysis provides useful insights into customer purchasing patterns and sales distribution. The analysis indicates that **female customers aged 26–35, particularly married women, represent a substantial share of the observed purchases**. From a geographic perspective, **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka** contribute significantly to overall sales. Customers working in **IT, Healthcare, and Aviation** also show relatively high purchase activity.

Overall, the findings suggest that **customer demographics, geographic location, and occupation are important dimensions for understanding purchasing behavior**. These insights can help businesses identify major customer segments, understand regional demand, and develop more targeted marketing and sales strategies.

However, these observations describe **patterns and associations in the dataset rather than causal relationships**. Further analysis, such as customer segmentation, statistical testing, or predictive modeling, could provide deeper insights into the factors associated with purchasing behavior.
